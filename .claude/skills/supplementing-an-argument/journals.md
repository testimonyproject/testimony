# Where to look, and what it costs to read

The journals and databases to search when researching a premise or auditing a rating, with how a
reader without an institution gets at each. Use it with step 3 (research) and step 4 (audit) of
`SKILL.md`, and from the `rating-auditor` and `source-verifier` agents.

**The access column is checked, not remembered.** Each fact below comes from the page named,
read on the date given. Where a page could not be read, the cell says "not verified": it does
not mean the journal is closed, only that nobody here has confirmed how it is opened. Never fill
a "not verified" cell from memory, a search snippet or Wikipedia. Read the publisher's own page
in a browser, and write down the URL and date.

## The sources behind each cell

- **NTA**: EBSCO's product page,
  <https://about.ebsco.com/products/research-databases/new-testament-abstracts-online>, and
  Boston College's news notice of spring 2026 (bc.edu, "New Testament Abstracts") that SBL takes
  over publication from 1 June 2026.
  Older pages naming CUA Press as subscription agent are out of date. Queries go to the SBL.
- **RBL**: <https://www.bookreviews.org/> says it "is now a part of the new SBL Central site"
  (10,000+ reviews since 1998). Reports of whether it is free conflict; check SBL Central.
- **TC**: moved to <https://scholarlypublishingcollective.org/sblpress/tc> in November 2025, by
  report of two blogs (Evangelical Textual Criticism; PaleoJudaica). Whether the older volumes
  remain at jbtc.org is not known.
- **CBQ**: <https://catholicbiblical.org/catholic-biblical-quarterly-cbq>: "Membership in CBA
  … includes a subscription to CBQ"; back issues from 1939 "available on-line without additional
  charge to CBA members only". It is also in the ATLASerials collection, which many seminary
  libraries hold. Individual subscriptions: <https://www.press.jhu.edu/cart/for-sale?oc=3483>.
- **JETS**: <https://etsjets.org/jets/jets-pdf-archive/>: "Visitors to the ETS website can
  access all but the most recent two full years of JETS." Volumes 12–66 are public there;
  volumes 1–11 were not listed as public. Membership includes the journal:
  <https://etsjets.org/membership/membership-applications-and-levels/>.
- **BBR**: prices from <https://www.psupress.org/journals/jnls_BBR.html> (add $26 shipping
  outside the US); membership includes the journal, <https://www.ibr-bbr.org/join>, and requires
  assent to IBR's confessional basis and the board's approval.
- **Tyndale Bulletin**: DOAJ's record (open access from 2021, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, no author fee);
  read it at <https://tyndalebulletin.org/>. How far back its archive reaches is not confirmed.
- **Christian Apologetics Journal**: <https://www.galaxie.com/volume/995> ("$5 per month or $50
  per year"), and <https://www.logos.com/product/4611/christian-apologetics-journal>. Whether it
  is still published is not stated.
- **The rest** (JBL, *Vetus Testamentum*, *Novum Testamentum*, JSOT, JSNT, IJST): only the
  publisher is confirmed, from Crossref's journal records. Read the publisher's page before
  saying anything about access.

## How to use it

- **Search NTA first** when the question is about a New Testament text: it abstracts the
  journals above and many more, so it shows what exists before anything is bought.
- **Prefer what anyone can read.** The library cites for a reader. A premise that rests on an
  open article (*Tyndale Bulletin*, JETS's archive) can be checked by anyone; one that rests on a
  paywalled article is still citable, but say on the page where it can be had.
- **List what has to be bought.** When research turns up a source you could not read, do not
  cite its contents from an abstract or a review. Report it to the user as a source to procure,
  with author, title, journal, year, volume and pages, and the access route from the table, so
  they can get the copy. Cite only what someone has read.
- **Abstracts and reviews are leads.** An NTA abstract or an RBL review says what a work argues;
  it is not the work. A premise cites the work itself, at a verified location.
